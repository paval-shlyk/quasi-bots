# Ground-Truth Sources

## 1. Алгоритмы и структуры данных
*   **Donald Knuth**, *The Art of Computer Programming (TAOCP)* — Фундаментальный труд.
    *   [Official Page](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/taocp.html)
*   **Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein**, *Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)* — Стандартный университетский учебник.
    *   [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262046305/introduction-to-algorithms/)
*   **Robert Sedgewick, Kevin Wayne**, *Algorithms* — Классический подход.
    *   [Online Course Materials](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/home/)

## 2. Криптография
*   **Bruce Schneier**, *Applied Cryptography* — Классика прикладной криптографии.
    *   [Author's Page](https://www.schneier.com/books/applied_cryptography/)
*   **Jonathan Katz, Yehuda Lindell**, *Introduction to Modern Cryptography* — Академический стандарт.
    *   [Publisher Page](https://www.crcpress.com/Introduction-to-Modern-Cryptography/Katz-Lindell/p/book/9781466570269)
*   **Niels Ferguson, Bruce Schneier, Tadayoshi Kohno**, *Cryptography Engineering*.
    *   [Author's Page](https://www.schneier.com/books/cryptography_engineering/)

## 3. Блокчейн
*   **Satoshi Nakamoto**, *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System* (Whitepaper).
    *   [PDF](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)
*   **Gavin Wood**, *Ethereum: A Secure Decentralised Generalised Transaction Ledger* (Yellow Paper).
    *   [PDF](https://ethereum.github.io/yellowpaper/paper.pdf)
*   **Andreas M. Antonopoulos**, *Mastering Bitcoin* / *Mastering Ethereum*.
    *   [Mastering Bitcoin (GitHub)](https://github.com/bitcoinbook/bitcoinbook)
    *   [Mastering Ethereum (GitHub)](https://github.com/ethereumbook/ethereumbook)
